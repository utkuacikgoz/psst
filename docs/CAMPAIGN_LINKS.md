# App Store campaign links

Each link opens Psst's App Store page and tells App Store Connect where the visit came from. Results: App Store Connect → Apps → Psst → Analytics → Acquisition → Campaigns. A campaign only appears once at least 5 different Apple Accounts have installed through it.

Use the link for the place you're posting. Campaign names (`ct`) are short, lowercase and at most 30 characters; to add a channel, copy a link and change only `ct`.

| Campaign | Where to use it | Link |
|---|---|---|
| `site-home` | psstapp.fun home page button (already on the site) | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=site-home&mt=8` |
| `site-invite` | invite pages, psstapp.fun/invite/… (already on the site) | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=site-invite&mt=8` |
| `reddit` | Reddit posts and comments | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=reddit&mt=8` |
| `tiktok` | TikTok bio and videos | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=tiktok&mt=8` |
| `instagram` | Instagram bio, Reels and stories | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=instagram&mt=8` |
| `x` | X / Twitter | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=x&mt=8` |
| `producthunt` | Product Hunt listing | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=producthunt&mt=8` |
| `showhn` | Show HN on Hacker News | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=showhn&mt=8` |
| `launch-sites` | other launch sites (Fazier, BuildHop, StartupBase, …) | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=launch-sites&mt=8` |
| `friends` | links you send friends and family yourself | `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6815764359?pt=129413467&ct=friends&mt=8` |

`pt=129413467` is the App Store Connect provider token; it is the same on every link and isn't secret.
