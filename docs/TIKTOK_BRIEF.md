# TikTok brief: @psst

A new account whose only job is to get people to install Psst and invite one person. Facts in videos must match the shipped app. Check "Only after 1.0.4" items before using them.

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Psst is a one-tap "thinking of you" button for your few people.

**The feeling we sell:** the small warm jolt of getting a "psst" from someone. It isn't productivity and it isn't chat. It's a tiny gesture with no pressure to reply.

**Angle that travels on TikTok:** "Yo is back, but sweet."
- It's absurdly simple, and that's the point.
- You can't write anything, so you can't say the wrong thing.

**Who it's for:**
1. Couples, especially long-distance.
2. Best-friend pairs and small friend groups (3–6 people).
3. Siblings, and parents with grown-up kids.

**Who it's not for:** people looking for a new social network. Never pitch followers, feeds or "meet people".

- **Avatar:** the app icon on its purple. Don't use a face; the brand is the cat and the colour bands.
- **Account type:** Business account. It unlocks the link in bio and analytics, but restricts some music: use TikTok's Commercial Music Library or original sound.
- **Our sound:** post the Psst whisper ("ps-st") as an original sound in the first video, and reuse it in every video so it becomes recognisable.

## 3. Content pillars

Aim for about 60% / 25% / 15% across the first 30 videos.

### A. The moment (60%)

Real-feeling micro-stories: a phone lights up with "psst" from someone. These are the main growth driver.
- She's in a meeting, the phone buzzes, it's a psst from him, and she psst-backs under the table.
- **SAME MOMENT:** both people tap within 10 seconds and get the full-screen SAME MOMENT. This is the best clip we have.
- Mum learns to psst.
- Long distance: a 3 am psst across time zones.

### B. The idea (25%)

Fast, punchy explainers.
- "Yo had one word. We have one tap."
- "An app that does exactly one thing" (screen recording, 7 seconds).
- "Things Psst doesn't have": a list with each item crossed off (feed, chat, typing, contacts upload, location).

### C. Behind the app (15%)

Solo-maker build-in-public content.
- "I built a Yo for people I love."
- Showing the cat design, the whisper sound being made, and the first real psst from a stranger.
- Real numbers when they're good.

## 5. Production rules

- **Film on two real iPhones.** Use real notifications, not mock-ups. Screen-record with the microphone on so the whisper is heard.
- **Captions on everything.** People watch muted, so the on-screen text has to tell the story without sound.
- **No faces needed.** Hands, phones and reactions are enough. If friends appear, get their OK first.
- **Batch it:** film 6–8 videos in one session per week.
- **Never fake it:**
  - no staged "reviews";
  - no claiming user numbers we don't have;
  - no "free unlimited" (only one Super Psst a day is free, and Psst+ is a one-time purchase);
  - never say "delivered" or "read". The app says "sent" and "seen".
- **Disclosure:** if a creator is ever paid or given Psst+ for a post, they must turn on TikTok's "Paid partnership" label.
- **Privacy:** never show a real person's invite code or a real account name without consent.

## 6. Cadence (first 4 weeks)

- **Weeks 1–2:**
  - 1 video a day, mostly pillar A, testing hooks #1–#8.
  - Reply to every comment within the first hour. Use video replies for good questions.
- **Weeks 3–4:**
  - Remake the 2 best performers with new people and settings ("same format, new story"). Keep 1 a day.
  - Add 1 build-in-public video a week.
- **Times:** post when your audience is online (TikTok Analytics → Followers). Start with 19:00–22:00 local time.

## 7. Measuring it

| What | Where | Target, first month |
|---|---|---|
| 2-second hold rate | TikTok Analytics, per video | > 60% |
| Average watch time | TikTok Analytics | > 70% of video length |
| Profile → link clicks | TikTok Analytics | rising week on week |
| Installs from TikTok | App Store Connect → App Analytics → Sources → Campaigns → `tiktok` | the real number; compare to Reddit / Instagram |
| Invites accepted | `docs/METRICS.md` daily metrics (`accepted_invites`) | should rise with installs. If not, the first invite isn't landing. |

**Each week:** kill hooks below a 40% hold rate, double down on the top 2, and note which pillar drove installs (not views).

## 10. Growth playbook

### The fixed format (use it for most videos)

1. **0–2 s:** a face reacting to a phone buzzing. The hook sits on screen as lowercase text, and the app isn't named yet.
2. **2–6 s:** a screen recording: a Psst arrives, they tap PSST BACK, and SAME MOMENT fills the screen.
3. **End:** on the reaction, then the CTA: "psst your person · link in bio".

Post 2–3 a day across 3–5 accounts: @psstapp, the founder's account, and friends' accounts. Most videos flop by design. You're hunting for the 1 in 30 that does 50×.

### Hooks to test first

| Hook | Audience |
|---|---|
| pov: you can't text him at work but he still knows | couples / long distance |
| we tapped at the exact same second 😳 | everyone (Same Moment: the clip nobody else can make) |
| my best friend and i have a secret button | best friends |
| this app has one button and i'm obsessed | curiosity |
| send this to the person you'd psst first | share bait (this is what spreads) |
| my mom learned to psst. it's over for me | family |
| sevgilime yazamıyorum ama… / aynı saniyede bastık / annem psst'ı öğrendi | Turkish |

### Own "long distance" before Tethered does

- **Search terms:** people already search "best apps for long distance couples" and "cute apps for couples". Put those exact words in captions and in on-screen text.
- **List video:** run "3 apps that saved our long distance" with Psst as #1, and make sure it actually is #1 in the video.

### Micro-creators

- **Who:** each month, 20–30 creators with 2k–30k followers: couples, best-friend pairs and Turkish university students.
- **Pay:** $20–50 per video for the fixed format, plus a free Psst+.
- **Disclosure:** every paid post must use TikTok's **Paid partnership** label (see §5).
- **Creator brief:** use the fixed format above, with two real phones and real notifications. Don't film real invite codes or other people's names without their OK, and keep the whisper sound on.

### The product is the distribution

- **Close every video on something to send.** End on "psst your person · link in bio".
- **Bio link:** the tracked `ct=tiktok` App Store link.
- **Pull in the second person:** a Psst link (psstapp.fun/p/…) brings them in.
- **One number to judge a video by:** invites accepted per install. A video only counts if people bring someone in.

### Respond and remake

- **Comments:** reply with video replies within the first hour.
- **Winners:** any video that beats 10× your median gets 5 remakes that week, with the same hook and new people.
- **Losers:** drop hooks that keep fewer than 40% of viewers past the first 2 seconds (§7).

### Turkey first

Puffy's developer is likely aiming at Turkey, and Psst is in Turkish from 1.0.6. Get there first with Turkish hooks, Turkish creators and Turkish captions. It's a smaller pond, so you can win it quickly.
