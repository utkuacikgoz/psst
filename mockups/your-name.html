Your name: options

Two things to pick: how you name yourself the first time (NM), and how you see and change it later in Settings (ED). The grey ones are what the app does today. Example name: Sam.

1 · Naming yourself (first launch)

Today the screen only says "YOUR NAME" over a band. All options keep one field and one button; they differ in how clearly they say who sees the name.

2 · Seeing and changing it later (Settings)

Today Settings shows an empty "New name" box; your current name isn't shown anywhere. Every option shows it.
