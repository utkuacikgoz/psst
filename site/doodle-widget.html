Doodle widget, answered.

Is the doodle widget free?

Yes. Sending doodles and the doodle on the Psst widget are free, with or without Psst+.

Do they need Psst?

Yes, and you can send the doodle first: Draw someone new gives them a one-person link, sealed until they join. Then it's the first thing they see.

How do they see it?

It fills their screen when they open Psst. If they've added the Psst widget, it sits on their Home Screen as a little polaroid until they psst you back.

Who can send me a Psst or a doodle?

Only people you've both said yes to: connecting takes an invite link or code. No strangers, no feed, no contacts upload. Hold anyone's band to block or report them.
