Questions, answered.

What is Psst?

Psst is a free iPhone app for the people you're close to. Tap someone's colour band and they get a whispered “psst” that means thinking of you. They can psst you back in one tap, even straight from the notification.

Is Psst free?

Yes. Pssting, replying, doodles, inviting people and one Super Psst a day are free, and so is the Control Center button. Psst+ is an optional one-time purchase: your word, unlimited Super Pssts, whisper sounds, widgets for your people, band colours and app icons.

What's a doodle?

Hold someone's band and choose Doodle to draw them something with one finger. It fills their screen when they open Psst, and if they've added the Psst widget it sits on their Home Screen as a little polaroid until they psst you back. Doodles are free and, like every Psst, deleted after 30 days.

Can I psst someone who isn't on Psst?

Yes. Tap the + band and choose Psst someone new or Draw someone new. They get a link that works for one person for 7 days. Your Psst or doodle stays sealed until they get Psst and join, then it's the first thing they see.

Who can send me a Psst?

Only people you've connected with. Connecting takes an invite link or code, and nothing is sent until you both say yes. Hold anyone's band to remove, block or report them.

What does Psst need from me?

Just a name to show your people. No email address, phone number or password, and Psst never collects your contacts, location or photos.

What do I need to use Psst?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later, and someone to psst.

How do I delete my account?

In Psst, open Settings and choose Delete account. Your account and its data are removed from our server straight away.
