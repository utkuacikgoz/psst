Long distance, answered.

Does Psst work across countries and time zones?

Yes. A Psst goes wherever they are, the moment you send it. Streak days follow each of your own phones' calendars.

Is it private?

Psst is just the two of you, or the few people you invite. No feed, no strangers, no contacts upload, no location.

Is Psst free?

Yes. Pssting, replying, doodles, inviting people and one Super Psst a day are free. Psst+ is an optional one-time purchase: your word, unlimited Super Pssts, whisper sounds, widgets for your people, band colours and app icons.

Who can send me a Psst or a doodle?

Only people you've both said yes to: connecting takes an invite link or code. No strangers, no feed, no contacts upload. Hold anyone's band to block or report them.
